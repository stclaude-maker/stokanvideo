About

I am Aleksandr Tokan, a video editor and motion designer. I started in 2005 as a news camera operator, including national TV channels in Russia, then moved into editing, post-production and multicamera live production for events and sports.

Today I live in Buenos Aires and work with production companies, brands, experts and content creators from Argentina, Russia and beyond — from corporate and training videos to reels with animated infographics and live streams.

I adapt to each client’s visual identity and production timeline. Clients point out that revisions are minimal.
